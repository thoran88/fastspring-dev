Add data-fsc-action

Adds a product to the cart. The default quantity is one unless it is changed separately with Update. Clicking Add again for a product already in the cart does not increment its quantity.

AttributeWhereValue
data-fsc-actionclickable element"Add"
data-fsc-item-path-valuesame elementproduct path, e.g. "prod-1"
Note: to add a product at a specific quantity in one click, pair this with a quantity control the way the Update demo does.
