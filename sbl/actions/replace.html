Replace data-fsc-action

Replaces one product already in the cart with another in a single click — the common pattern for upsell offers ("swap your plan for the Pro plan").

AttributeWhereValue
data-fsc-actionclickable element"Replace"
data-fsc-item-path-valuesame elementthe new product being added, e.g. "prod-2"
data-fsc-driver-item-path-valuesame elementthe existing cart item being replaced, e.g. "prod-1"
Note: the driver item (prod-1) must be in the cart first, and it needs a Product Offer configured in the FastSpring dashboard (Catalog → prod-1 → Related Offers → Upsell, pointing at prod-2) — without that relationship, Replace silently falls back to just adding the target item instead of swapping. That relationship is now configured on this storefront.
