recognize fastspring.builder.*
fastspring.builder.recognize(payload, secureKey?)

Prefills unauthenticated customer information (email, name) into the checkout session.

Note: this stores data in the browser session — the values won't appear in the builder's own data object, but they will prefill the checkout form when it opens.

Live demo

Source

fastspring.builder.recognize({
  email: document.getElementById('rec-email').value,
  firstName: document.getElementById('rec-first').value,
  lastName: document.getElementById('rec-last').value
});
fastspring.builder.checkout();
